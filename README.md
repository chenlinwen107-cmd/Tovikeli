Tovikeli
ACG · 兴趣社区 · 创作者 · 社交平台
Tovikeli is a modern social platform source-code product designed for ACG communities, interest-based communities, creators, and general social platforms.
Tovikeli provides a practical foundation for developers and teams who want to build, customize, or deploy their own social community platform.

Core Features
Tovikeli is designed as a restrained, modern social-platform source-code template rather than a gamification-heavy community product.
Core capabilities include:
User accounts and authentication
Social feed
Posts and content publishing
Post editing and deletion
Image and video media
User profiles
Social interactions
Comments
Private messaging
Questions and answers
Topics and content discovery
Search
Creator-oriented content features
Administrative functionality
The platform can serve as a foundation for building:
ACG communities
Interest-based communities
Creator platforms
Content communities
General-purpose social platforms
